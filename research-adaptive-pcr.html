Research area · Molecular Diagnostics

Adaptive PCR

PCR machines fail in the field because they blindly follow fixed temperature programs. We use mirror-image L-DNA to optically monitor primer annealing and product melting in real time, letting the instrument react to what is actually happening.

Overview

Why this work matters

Adaptive PCR combines real-time optical feedback with thermal cycling so the instrument can adapt to the chemistry instead of relying only on a pre-programmed temperature schedule. The lab has used mirror-image L-DNA reporters to track primer annealing and product melting without interfering with the natural DNA reaction.

Research focus

Thermal feedback

Our goal is to translate rigorous molecular and physical principles into diagnostic workflows that can function with fewer instruments, fewer infrastructure assumptions, and clearer readouts for real-world use.
