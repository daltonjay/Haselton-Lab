Coffee Ring & Evaporative Diagnostics

When a drop evaporates on a flat surface, particles naturally concentrate at the edge — the coffee ring effect. We exploit this physics to make biomarkers visible to the naked eye without any active instrument.

← Back to all research areas
Overview

Why this work matters

Evaporation can become a passive microfluidic engine. By controlling where suspended particles and biomolecular complexes accumulate as a droplet dries, the lab explores instrument-free readouts that concentrate signal into visible patterns.

Research focus

Passive concentration

Our goal is to translate rigorous molecular and physical principles into diagnostic workflows that can function with fewer instruments, fewer infrastructure assumptions, and clearer readouts for real-world use.
