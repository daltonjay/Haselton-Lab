L-DNA as a Molecular Mirror

L-DNA is chemically identical to natural DNA but biologically inert — it can shadow PCR reactions without disrupting them.

← Back to all research areas
Overview

Why this work matters

Mirror-image L-DNA provides a chemically familiar but biologically orthogonal layer for observing and validating molecular reactions. In the lab’s work, these reporters can follow amplification behavior, help distinguish intended products from background, and support logic-based molecular sensing concepts.

Research focus

Orthogonal sensing

Our goal is to translate rigorous molecular and physical principles into diagnostic workflows that can function with fewer instruments, fewer infrastructure assumptions, and clearer readouts for real-world use.
