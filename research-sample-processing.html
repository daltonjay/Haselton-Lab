Research area · Sample Processing

Field-Ready Sample Processing

Extraction is the step that most often breaks in the field. We strip it down to what is necessary — magnetic beads, a transfer pipette, steel wool, and a magnet.

Overview

Why this work matters

Sample preparation can determine whether a molecular test succeeds outside a conventional laboratory. The lab develops simple concentration and extraction workflows that reduce equipment, power, reagent, and cold-chain requirements while still recovering target nucleic acids from challenging clinical samples.

Research focus

Low-resource extraction

Our goal is to translate rigorous molecular and physical principles into diagnostic workflows that can function with fewer instruments, fewer infrastructure assumptions, and clearer readouts for real-world use.
