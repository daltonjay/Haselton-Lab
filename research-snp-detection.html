Research area · Global Health

SNP Detection Without Sequencing

Sequencing is too expensive and infrastructure-heavy for most of the world. We develop oligonucleotide ligation assays and melt-based screens that identify clinically important single-nucleotide variants at the point of care.

Overview

Why this work matters

This research area focuses on targeted genetic tests for variants that matter clinically, without requiring full sequencing infrastructure. Oligonucleotide ligation and melt-based approaches are used to discriminate single-base changes associated with drug resistance, pathogen variants, and treatment selection.

Research focus

Variant discrimination

Our goal is to translate rigorous molecular and physical principles into diagnostic workflows that can function with fewer instruments, fewer infrastructure assumptions, and clearer readouts for real-world use.
