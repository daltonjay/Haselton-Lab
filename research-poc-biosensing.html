Point-of-Care Biosensing Platforms

We apply the same low-resource design principles to a range of clinical targets, including skin sampling, paper devices, smartphone spectroscopy, and enhanced lateral-flow assays.

Overview

Why this work matters

This portfolio applies low-resource engineering principles across several sensing formats. Projects pair practical sampling with compact readout strategies, including microneedles, paper-based devices, smartphones, and affinity-enhanced lateral flow, with an emphasis on clinical usability outside centralized laboratories.

Research focus

Accessible platforms

Our goal is to translate rigorous molecular and physical principles into diagnostic workflows that can function with fewer instruments, fewer infrastructure assumptions, and clearer readouts for real-world use.
